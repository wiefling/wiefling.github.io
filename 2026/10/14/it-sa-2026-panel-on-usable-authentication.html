it-sa 2026: Shaping the Future of Customer Authentication With Bitkom and BSI

Oct 14, 2026 •
Stephan Wiefling
• 1 min
cyber security usability usable security panel customer it-sa

See you in Nürnberg! I’ll be part of the Bitkom panel on usable customer authentication at it-sa 2026, Europe’s biggest cyber security fair.

Together with Steffen Waurick (Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI)), Felix Kuhlenkamp (Bitkom), and Frank-Michael Kamm (D+G), we will discuss specific challenges, potential solutions, and the outcomes of an industry dialogue that will contribute to secure authentication.

Let me know if you’ll be there, too. I’m looking forward to some interesting conversations!

📅 Tuesday, 27 October 2026 | 13:00 – 13:30h

📍 Knowledge Forum B, Booth 7A-206, it-sa Expo

Making Science Accessible: My Research as Blog Posts

Jun 8, 2026 •
Stephan Wiefling
• 1 min
academia publications research

For many years, I’ve published scientific articles on various topics. And now, it’s time to make them more accessible for everyone.

Instead of downloading a PDF file, you can read them directly on the blog of this website. No extra steps are needed, and they work smoothly in any web browser.

More Advantages:

  • Quick reading experience: No PDF is needed, so you just need a web browser.
  • Simple discovery: Every post is tagged, so you can use them to find articles by topic.
  • Better accessibility: I made sure that every image includes descriptive alt text, making these posts usable for screen readers and improving readability for everyone.

I would like to give a big thank you to the open-source tool Marker, which made converting documents into blog-friendly formats much easier.

Like to see how it looks like? Just try it out.

What do you think? Have you found this format helpful? If so, I’d love to hear your thoughts in the comments below. What would make these articles even more useful for you?
